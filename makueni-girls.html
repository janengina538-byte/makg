About Us

A home for learning and growth

Makueni Girls High School is a public, boarding secondary school dedicated entirely to the education of girls. Nestled in Makueni County in Kenya's Eastern region, the school has built a reputation for discipline, academic focus and character formation.


We believe education is a partnership between the school, parents and the wider community. Every learner who passes through our gates is given the structure, support and encouragement to discover her own potential — academically, socially and personally.

Academics

Learning built for the future

Competency-Based Curriculum

Our teaching aligns with Kenya's CBC/CBE framework, balancing core academic subjects with practical, skills-based learning.

Sciences & Languages

Strong foundations in mathematics, sciences, languages and humanities, taught by a dedicated and experienced teaching staff.

Mentorship & Guidance

Regular guidance and counselling sessions help every student build confidence, resilience and a sense of direction.

School Life

Beyond the classroom

Boarding Life

A structured routine of study, rest and community living that builds independence and discipline.

Sports & Games

Athletics, ball games and inter-house competitions keep students active and build team spirit.

Clubs & Societies

Debate, drama, music and Christian Union give students room to explore talents outside academics.

Community & Values

A culture of respect, integrity and service, instilled through daily school life and leadership roles.

Admissions

How to join us

Check Placement

Confirm placement through the Ministry of Education's national school placement portal, or enquire directly with our administration.

Submit Documents

Bring your admission letter, birth certificate, KCPE/primary school results slip, and passport photos on reporting day.

Reporting & Orientation

New students report with the required boarding items and go through an orientation programme with staff and prefects.
